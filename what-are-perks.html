What Are Perks?

A simple explanation of perks, where they can appear, and why they differ from one GRAM to another.

What perks are

Perks are benefits, access, or offers connected to a GRAM. They can be tied to a specific piece, a collection state, or an associated partner experience.

Are all perks the same?

No. Different GRAMs can have different perks, and some may have none. The perks available depend on that specific GRAM and its associated setup.

Where perks can appear

Perks can appear on the current GRAM or inside your collection, depending on the context.

  • On the current GRAM page
  • Inside My Collection
  • Alongside partner or experience-related actions

Having a perk vs using a perk

Seeing a perk means it is associated with your GRAM or collection state. Using it is a separate step.
