Notifications

What notifications mean, where to see them, and how they differ from transfers.

What notifications are

Notifications are activity updates related to your GRAM account and collection. They can let you know about changes, reminders, or actions that may need your attention.

Where to see notifications

You can review notifications in your inbox or notifications area in the platform. Siggy may also point you there when something new is waiting.

What notifications can include

  • Collection-related updates
  • Perk-related activity
  • Transfer-related activity
  • Other account or GRAM updates

Notifications vs transfers

A notification is an update. A transfer is a specific ownership action. Some transfer events may appear through notifications, but they are not the same thing.

Important: If you need to approve or review ownership movement, open transfers. If you only want to check what is new, start with notifications or inbox.
